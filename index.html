About

Parts that fit together, carry load and survive their environment

That is the work I enjoy most. In my first term at Guelph I joined Gryphon Aerospace and both the solid and liquid rocket teams at Gryphon Rocketry, and took on real engineering work: selecting cameras and designing a two-axis gimbal for a competition UAV, and choosing recovery hardware and modelling the airframe for a high-power rocket. On my own time I designed a 3D-printed folding lap desk, from the first sketch to a full drawing set.
